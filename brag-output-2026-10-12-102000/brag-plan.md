# Brag plan: BeeTip

## What is this app?
BeeTip lets members of the BINUS Alam Sutera community ask for a purchase or offer a jastip trip through a mobile marketplace.

## The angle
The moment is small and familiar: a neighbor is already going to AEON, and someone nearby needs a drink. The film follows the real BeeTip screens from the marketplace to a request form, then to an existing order and its chat. The AEON demo listing currently shows "Kapasitas penuh"; the cut keeps that status visible and does not suggest a new order was accepted.

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
Open close on the real marketplace board and land the exact in-app line "Lagi ke AEON?" in large olive type. A quick card push reveals the BeeTip mark and the "Titip Chatime" listing.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The board visibly separates "Jasa titip tersedia" from "Minta dibelikan".
- The AEON listing shows its existing title, service fee, purchase capacity, verification, location, timing, and full-capacity status.
- The create-listing screen moves from "Minta dibelikan" into the real request form.
- The existing BTP-001 order shows "Dalam perjalanan"; its associated chat carries the seeded demo message.

## Outro / punchline
Land on the BeeTip hexagon and wordmark, then hold the app's tagline: "Titip belanja, sekalian jalan."

## User flow worth showing
Browse the board -> open the AEON trip listing -> open "Buat listing" and choose "Minta dibelikan" -> see the request form -> see an existing in-progress order and its chat. The form is shown at its real starting state; the video does not claim a new request or order was submitted.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: warm neighborhood mini-trailer, with restrained cinematic scale and real mobile UI
- Interpretation: Use short camera moves, clean cuts, and readable holds. Keep the oat canvas, honey accents, sage panels, and the app's Geist typography.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 28 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#F7F3E9`
- Accent: `#F3AE4E`
- Text: `#2E3028`
- Display font: Geist Sans
- Body font: Geist Sans
- Strongest visual element: the mobile marketplace board and its warm, rounded listing cards

## Share copy (draft)
BeeTip. Titip belanja, sekalian jalan.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm cinematic support
- Music: bundled "Happy Beats / Business Moves" vol. 12, upbeat and human
- Music treatment: start under the hook at 0s, keep it low, fade out over the final 0.7s
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue file. Keep the opening accent at 0.56s. The detail click lands at 6.83s, the listing choice click at 9.91s, and the brand bell at 24.43s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; a honey highlight and the final hexagon breathe slightly with the music
- SFX posture: sparse; soft interface clicks for the selection and request-form reveal, one restrained bell on the brand landing
- Audio-coupled moments: hook reveal, listing detail reveal, request choice, final logo landing
- Restraint rule: no voiceover, payment sounds, or celebratory cues that imply a completed payment or newly accepted order

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 - AEON hook - 2.8s
Use a close crop of the real marketplace screenshot. Bring in "Lagi ke AEON?" at 0.56s, then reveal the "Titip Chatime" card edge. Use the exact existing app copy. No invented shop, user, or order.
Sequential/interaction: yes - one quick push into the first listing, then hold the hook.
Audio intent: a short warm swell under the card reveal.
Audio-coupled idea: the hook lands on the first strong beat.
Transition mood: clean -> Scene 2

### Scene 2 - The board and listing - 7s
Show the full marketplace screen first so both listing badges read. Move into the actual "Titip Chatime" detail screen and hold its existing fee, capacity, verification, AEON location, delivery window, and "Kapasitas penuh" state. Use the values rendered in the screenshot, including IDR formatting.
Sequential/interaction: yes - board to detail, with one click cue at the detail reveal.
Audio intent: light rhythmic support without crowding the UI.
Audio-coupled idea: small labels can arrive on the beat, then hold together.
Transition mood: soft -> Scene 3

### Scene 3 - Start a request - 7s
Show the real "Buat listing" choice screen, then the actual form reached after choosing "Minta dibelikan". Keep the form unsubmitted. The app's own line "Sedang ke AEON? Buka titipan untuk teman yang ingin ikut belanja." is visible as part of the choice screen.
Sequential/interaction: yes - tap "Minta dibelikan", then reveal the request form.
Audio intent: a quiet click marks the choice; music keeps the pace moving.
Audio-coupled idea: click at 9.91s, form reveal near 13.01s.
Transition mood: clean -> Scene 4

### Scene 4 - Order and chat - 5.6s
Show the existing BTP-001 order and "Dalam perjalanan" status, then its existing order chat. These are local seeded demo records. Do not add payment, escrow, live tracking, or settlement cues.
Sequential/interaction: yes - order detail to its associated chat.
Audio intent: warm and matter-of-fact.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft transition click, no success sound.
Transition mood: slow dissolve -> Scene 5

### Scene 5 - BeeTip - 5.6s
Use the real hexagon mark and wordmark treatment from the app, with the exact tagline "Titip belanja, sekalian jalan." Let the final card settle and hold.
Sequential/interaction: yes - hexagon, BeeTip wordmark, tagline.
Audio intent: gentle resolve, music fades out.
Audio-coupled idea: final wordmark lands near 24.43s, with a soft bell.
Transition mood: warm fade to end

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, warm, cinematic
**Audio summary:** A light, human music bed carries a quick marketplace reveal into a calm, friendly brand close, with only a few motion-matched sounds.
