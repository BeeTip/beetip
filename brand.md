# BeeTip Brand

## Direction

BeeTip is a warm neighborhood marketplace for the BINUS Alam Sutera community. It should feel like a trusted campus noticeboard brought into a thoughtful, modern mobile app.

## Palette

- Canvas: `#F7F3E9`, a soft oat background.
- Surface: `#FFFCF6`, warm paper for cards and navigation.
- Primary: `#F3AE4E`, honey amber for primary actions and highlights.
- Ink: `#2E3028`, deep olive charcoal for headings and body copy.
- Muted: `#706D62`, readable secondary copy.
- Line: `#E5DED0`, quiet separators and borders.
- Supporting colors: sage `#DDE9D9`, persimmon `#E9A17A`, and pale honey `#F7E4BD`.
- Success and warning states use dark, high-contrast foregrounds with pale tinted backgrounds.

## Typography

Use the existing self-hosted Geist Sans. Keep headings compact and confident, body copy conversational, and monetary values easy to scan. Do not introduce decorative typefaces into transactional screens.

## Imagery and interface

Use the existing local food, drink, and shopping illustrations. Pair them with soft category colors, generous whitespace, rounded paper surfaces, thin warm borders, and restrained shadows. Design for one-handed mobile use first; let the desktop layout add breathing room without changing the task hierarchy.

## Voice

Write concise Indonesian copy in a friendly, practical tone. Make the next action clear. Keep purchase budgets, jastip fees, verification, timing, and privacy boundaries explicit. Never imply that demo data, manual payment records, or community verification guarantee settlement or endorsement.
